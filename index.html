How the risk score works

Corporate/LLC ownership % (35%) — share of residential parcels owned by LLCs, corporations, or investment entities.

Median days on market (30%) — how fast homes go under contract. Fewer days = hotter market.

Sale-to-list ratio (20%) — homes selling above list price signal bidding war conditions.

Risk score 0–100 — higher score means more competition and greater chance of being outbid. Weights renormalize when a factor is missing.
