How the risk score works

Six market signals — corporate ownership, days on market, sale-to-list ratio, % sold above ask, months of supply, and price-to-income ratio — are each normalized and weighted.

Score 0–100 — higher means more competition from cash and institutional buyers. Weights renormalize automatically when a factor is missing for a ZIP.
